By Age

Every decade, reimagined

16–25
Discovery Era
  • Experiment freely — bold choices now
  • Build 2–3 quality basics first
  • Cool tones: ice blue, slate, silver
  • Budget: $80–$200 per outfit
  • Shop: ASOS, Zara, H&M, Uniqlo
26–40
Building Era
  • Curate intentionally — quality over quantity
  • Capsule wardrobe: 12–15 pieces
  • Cool tones: navy, charcoal, teal
  • Budget: $200–$450 per outfit
  • Shop: Banana Republic, COS, J.Crew
41–64
Mastery Era
  • Timeless over trendy, always
  • Quality fabrics signal authority
  • Cool tones: midnight, steel, lavender
  • Budget: $400–$900+ per outfit
  • Shop: Ralph Lauren, Brooks Brothers, Nordstrom
